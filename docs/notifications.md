# Browser notifications

A tech can have Floorbit show browser notifications on their phone or laptop. Each person turns
them on for their own browser: **user menu > Notifications** on any of the newer pages. The
setting is kept in the browser, so it also covers the floor plan editor in the same browser.

What can notify, each with its own switch:

- **A job sent to me:** an incident dispatched to your account (see [Dispatch](dispatch.md)).
- **Every new incident:** for accounts that can see the incident board.
- **A switch going down:** a monitored switch going offline.

Notifications only arrive while a Floorbit tab is open, even in the background: they come from
the same live updates the pages use, with no push service and nothing stored on the server. A
notification that several open tabs would show appears once. Clicking it opens the incident
board, your jobs or the floor plan. The texts follow the language chosen in Floorbit.

If the browser blocks notifications for the site, allow them in its site settings and switch them
on again.

## Quiet hours

Tick **Quiet hours** and set **From** and **To** (this device's local time; they may run past
midnight, like 22:00 to 08:00). During them new incidents and switches going down stay quiet.
**Jobs sent to me still come through** is on by default, so a job dispatched to you at 3 AM still
reaches you; untick it for full quiet. Turning notifications off altogether is the switch at the
top.
