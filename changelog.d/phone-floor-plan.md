### Changed

- Floor plan on phones: the plan takes the whole screen. A floating search shows the online count,
  a small switch flips between map, list and topology, and a dock at the bottom opens Layers,
  Problems (with the filters), saved views and a More menu (hall, big screen, export, edit). The
  edit tool rail no longer shows while looking at the plan on a phone.
- Rows are named by small grey letters at their start, the size of the seat numbers, instead of
  boxed block tags; a block with problems shows a small red count at its corner.
- The keyboard shortcuts button shows only where there is a keyboard and mouse.
