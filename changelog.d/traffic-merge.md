### Changed

- **Services** is about health only: is each service up. What the hall uses moved to **Traffic**,
  which now has an **Overview** (the live bandwidth numbers and what the traffic is made of, on one
  page), **Games and downloads** (what each game fetched, with the LAN cache's numbers below it),
  **DNS**, Applications, Speed tests and Flow lookup. The Stats and LAN cache pages are gone; the
  cache machine shows in the LAN cache row's panel on Services, and Replay and the Event report are
  under Events.
