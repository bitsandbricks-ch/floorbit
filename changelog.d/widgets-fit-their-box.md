### Added

- Dashboard tiles fit their box: small (a number and its state), medium (the rows that fit and
  "+N more", or a graph) or large (tables page, sort and filter inside the tile; graphs show axes,
  maximum and average). Graphs show every line's value on hover. "Show as" and "Size" in the pane
  settings change how a tile is drawn.
- New tiles: switch states (down first), open incidents and traffic over time.
- Plugins can send a `series` widget: up to four lines over time.
