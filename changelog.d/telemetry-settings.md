### Changed

- Telemetry is set up on the **Telemetry** tab of a device, a group, a tag or All devices: on, off
  or inherit; **Automatic** or chosen ways to collect; MAC tables, VLANs, the MDT node, the agent,
  the address and the ports, each showing what applies when left empty and where it comes from.
  Groups, tags and All devices get tabs like a device's page.
- What a device leaves open now follows its software and interfaces: MAC tables per VLAN on Cisco
  IOS and IOS-XE read the VLANs on its interfaces, and Cisco MDT uses the device's name as its node.
- The **Telemetry** page has a summary, a device list you can search and filter (with problems,
  never heard), and tabs for the collector and the senders. Each device opens its own Telemetry tab
  with Test now, Raw data and Device setup.
- Pages use the full width of the window instead of a fixed column.
- The product spec names the core parts (agents, telemetry, seating plan and mapping, inventory,
  observability, services and monitoring), which always come first.
