### Added

- Seats view: `/plan` shows every seat of a hall as online, offline or unknown, each with its own
  shape so it reads in every theme and for colour-blind eyes, with marks for check-in, problems
  and match seats. Three zoom levels (block totals, states, seat numbers), a filter, seat search,
  and one inspector per seat that shows nicks, IPs and MACs only with `seats.view`.
  `?screen=1` for big screens. See `docs/seats.md`.
