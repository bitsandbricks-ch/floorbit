### Added

- Backups to an S3 bucket (Amazon S3, MinIO, Garage, Backblaze B2 and other S3 stores): uploaded on a schedule (every 24 hours by default) and with Back up now, the oldest deleted beyond a set number, and listed on Settings > Backup and restore to restore or download. With a passphrase, each backup is encrypted before it leaves. The access keys and passphrase are stored encrypted and never shown again.
