### Added

- Widget gallery at `/widgets`: every widget live with your data, filtered by area, opened big or added to a dashboard in one click.
- Ready-made dashboards: Overview, Service health, Network, Services, Traffic, Power load and the public Event in numbers.
- New widgets for Services, Power load, Network status, Endpoints and the event in numbers. A widget pane can be drawn big, and every widget opens big from the dashboard.
- `?rotate=a,b,c&every=30` on a dashboard's address moves through several dashboards on a timer.

### Changed

- Service health, Stats, Traffic, DNS, Games, Wi-Fi and Speed tests are widgets now, not pages of their own. Put them on a dashboard or use the ready-made ones.

### Removed

- The tile dashboard (`/dashboard`) and its saved layouts, `/screen`, the `/wall` page and `?screen=1` on Health and Stats. Dashboards, the gallery and `?rotate=` do their job. The Tiles and Page panes are gone from the dashboard editor.
