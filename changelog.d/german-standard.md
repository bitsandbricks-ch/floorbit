### Added

- German for Germany and Austria next to Swiss German: pick "Deutsch (Deutschland, Österreich)" in
  the account menu (it writes "ß"). Browsers set to de-DE or de-AT get it by default.
- Error messages from the server come in German when the app is in German. A CI check makes sure
  new messages get a German text; the ones still in English are listed in
  `floorbit/locales/untranslated.txt`.
