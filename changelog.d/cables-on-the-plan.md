### Added

- The floor plan editor draws the cables: network cables from switch ports to seats and uplinks on the
  Network layer, power from distribution boards on the Electrical layer, coloured by copper, fiber or DAC
  and thicker for faster links.
- Automatic cabling draws every seat whose port is known, routed along its seat block. It is on by default
  and can be turned off under Settings > Cabling for venues that patch through panels.
- The Cable tool (C) draws a network cable from a switch to a seat, a whole seat block or another switch, or
  a power cable from a distribution board to a seat block or switch.
- Cables can be routed by hand: click a selected cable to add a bend, drag it, double-click to remove it; round
  or square corners. Hand-routed cables keep their bends when the seat behind the port changes.
- Patch panels can be put on the plan; cables that name one run through it.
