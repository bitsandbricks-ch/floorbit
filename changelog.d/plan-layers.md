### Added

- **Layers on the Seats view:** one **Layers** menu stacks network, electrical, traffic, firewalls, Wi-Fi access points, safety, open incidents, check-in and services over the seats. Each layer has its own switch and strength, presets (Overview, Network, Electrical, Ops) set several at once, and the address keeps the choice.
- **Marks on a layer:** labels, walls, zones and icons can sit on the Safety, Network, Electrical or Services layer; new icons for fire extinguishers, escape routes, assembly points, servers, routers, access points, power and sockets. Attendee pages show only the overview and safety marks.

### Changed

- The Traffic, Power and Firewalls switches above the Seats view moved into the Layers menu; old links still work.
