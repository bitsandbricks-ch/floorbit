### Added

- A floor plan template library (`/templates`): save the floor plan, or one hall, as a template with
  versions; rename, duplicate, delete; download and import templates as files to share with another LAN;
  start from one, replacing the whole plan or one hall (audit-logged). Templates never hold check-ins,
  nicks, IPs, MACs, serials, secrets or accounts, and imports are cut down to the layout. See `docs/templates.md`.
