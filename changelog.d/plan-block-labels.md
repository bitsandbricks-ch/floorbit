### Fixed

- On a zoomed-out floor plan and in dashboard hall plans, blocks without a name (or with the same name) showed the first block's online and problem counts. Each block now shows its own, and unnamed blocks are labelled by their rows.
- Block labels on a zoomed-out plan no longer stack on top of each other or on neighbouring blocks: a label goes above its block, else beside it, and is left out if there is no room. Blocks with problems get their label first.
