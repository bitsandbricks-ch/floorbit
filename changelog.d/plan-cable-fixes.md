### Fixed

- Cables on the floor plan follow a moved switch or seat block at once, instead of after the next refresh.
- Seat cables visibly end at their seat: a dot marks where each plugs in, and cables to a block's far row
  go round the end of the block instead of disappearing under the near row.

### Changed

- Where three or more seat cables share a run, the plan draws them as one band with the number of cables on it.
