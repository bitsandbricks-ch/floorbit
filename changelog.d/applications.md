### Added

- **Applications** (Traffic › Applications): the games, launchers and site groups Floorbit recognises
  in DNS, traffic and the LAN cache, built from the open lists uklans/cache-domains and
  v2fly/domain-list-community. Add domains, ports, server addresses and Steam depots to any of them,
  add your own, or hide one.
- **Games played** on Games: games by title, from DNS, traffic, the event's game servers and the LAN
  cache, under the five-seat rule.

### Changed

- Games lists every launcher cache-domains knows (Rockstar, Nintendo, PlayStation, ...), not eight.
  Windows updates is a site group now instead of an "other cache".
- The agent's flow collector names traffic to the event's game servers and to the applications'
  ports and addresses.
