### Changed

- A seat block is now a list of rows. Each row has its own number of seats, its own space above it (the aisle), and lines up at the start, middle or end of the block. Rows can be added, reordered and taken out in the block's panel; circuits, seat walks and dashboards keep the same row when rows are reordered.
- Rows don't have to be straight: a row can be an arc, a U round a table, an L or a path, with the seats turned along it, and can show which side people sit on. Gaps for fiber rolls are set per row.
- Merging stacked blocks now works whatever their rows are like.
- Existing plans, templates and backups are moved to rows once, keeping every seat's name and place.
