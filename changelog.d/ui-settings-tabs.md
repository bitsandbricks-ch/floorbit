### Changed

- Settings is one page with tabs: General, People, Alerts, Tenants and System. Users, roles and
  teams, sign-in providers, API tokens, webhooks, features, sidebar sections, the audit log and
  tenants open inside their tab, at the same addresses as before.
- Plugins has three tabs: Installed, Marketplace and Updates, which lists the installed plugins with
  a newer version. NetBox and LibreNMS are set up on their plugin pages.
- The sidebar's Floorbit group is shorter: Agents, Plugins, Settings and Telemetry.
