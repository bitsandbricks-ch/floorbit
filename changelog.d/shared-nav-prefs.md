### Added

- **Customise sidebar** in the account menu: pin pages, move them up and down or into another
  group, put away what you never use under "More", rename headings and make groups of your own.
  The arrangement is kept with your account.

### Changed

- Your favourites carry over as the pinned pages at the top of the sidebar. The group of pages
  not sorted in yet is now called "Other".
