### Added

- Alignment guides in the floor plan editor: a dragged seat block snaps to the edges, middles and seat rows of
  other blocks and to equal spacing between two blocks, with lines and the spacing in seats. Hold Alt to
  place freely.
- Seat blocks can be grouped (Ctrl+G, Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups): a click selects the whole group, which moves,
  turns and copies as one, while each block keeps its seat names.
- Merge seat blocks next to each other into one: stacked blocks get more rows, blocks side by side carry the
  seat numbers on. Space between them becomes a gap.

### Fixed

- Saving several seat blocks at once (a group, a turn of a selection) could lose one of the changes.
