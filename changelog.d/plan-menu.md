### Added

- The floor plan editor has a **Plan** menu in its top bar: save the hall or the whole plan as a template, start from a template, place a preset, versions of the hall and the halls. An empty hall offers to start from a template.
- Presets: select items on the plan (seat blocks, walls, zones, text, icons, patch panels, power boards), right-click and save them together as a preset, then place it again in any hall or event, like a paste.
