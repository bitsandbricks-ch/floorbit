### Added

- **Labels, walls, zones and icons on the floor plan:** the editor's new **Draw** tool (**D**) adds text, walls (solid or dashed), named zones and icons (exit, WC, bar, first aid, stage, arrows and more). They show on the Seats view, the big screens and the attendee hall view too.
- **Example hall:** one click in the Halls dialog adds a ready hall with rows of seats, a standing row, walls, an entrance, zones and icons, to try things out.
