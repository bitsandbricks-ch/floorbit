### Changed

- Switches have their own icon, a small switch box with ports (lit when online, a cross when offline), and are
  always drawn over seat blocks and marks in the editor, on the Seats view and on the kiosk.

### Added

- Bring to front, Bring forward, Send backward and Send to back for seat blocks and for labels, walls, zones
  and icons, from the right-click menu or Ctrl+] and Ctrl+[ (with Shift: all the way).
