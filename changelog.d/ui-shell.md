### Added

- A new frame around every crew page: a top bar with where you are, "Go to" (Ctrl K) and a bell
  that opens what needs a look, a status line at the bottom, and the event picker at the top of
  the sidebar.
- Overview (`/overview`): the Overview dashboard inside the app, as the crew's start page.
- Any dashboard opens inside the app at `/dashboards/view/<id>`, and **Pin to sidebar** puts it
  with your favourites. `/dashboards/<id>` stays the big-screen view.

### Changed

- The sidebar is grouped: Live, Inventory, Monitoring, Seats, Event and Floorbit. Groups stay
  open and fold with a click on their name; folded to icons, the sidebar shows every page.
- Page headers are quieter, with less space around the page.
