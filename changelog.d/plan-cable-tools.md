### Added

- A seat cable on the floor plan shows its port: solid while the port is up, dashed when it is down,
  dotted when it isn't read. Cables only read from the switches (not typed in) are drawn fainter.
- The side panel of a selected cable changes its ends right there: switch and port, and seat.
- Cables can be drawn straight (end to end), angled or rounded, from the side panel. Straight keeps
  the bends, so angled brings them back.
