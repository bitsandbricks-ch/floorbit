### Added

- A right-click menu on the floor plan (long press on touch screens, Menu key or Shift+F10 on the
  keyboard) with what you can do with the seat, switch, block or empty floor under the pointer:
  seat details, edit the seat mapping, report an incident, bounce the port, show ports, show in
  inventory, add here, and more. Entries only show when your role allows them. See
  `docs/context-menu.md`.

### Changed

- Seat mapping and Inventory take `?q=` to open with a search filled in.
