### Changed

- Monitoring > Telemetry lists each device with its state, the same way as an agent's Devices tab.
  A device's problems sit in its row: the kind, where (the whole device, one VLAN or one table),
  since when and how often, then what to check; the collector's own line opens on demand. Filter by
  agent and by collector. The problems card counts devices that never reported, too.
