### Added

- Topology (Network › Topology): the links between switches with their cable type (copper Cat5e/6/6a, fiber single-mode or multi-mode, DAC) and planned speed (1 to 100G), drawn from the core down. Floorbit reads each link's ports and flags a link that is down or came up slower than planned. Neighbours the switches see by LLDP or CDP that no link explains yet can be added with one click (LibreNMS and the demo data offer them).
