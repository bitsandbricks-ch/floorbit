### Changed

- An agent's page has a cleaner header: its role in its group (primary or standby) next to its state, and the actions Check now, Roll key and More (settings, key and certificate, download the CA, revoke).
- The overview says who takes over when the agent stops, or whose work it does while it is away, and lists its latest events.
- New tabs on an agent's page: **Logs** (what the audit log holds about it: keys, certificates, settings, and the updates of its plugins and of the agent itself) and **Key and certificate** (moved out of Settings).
