### Changed

- Topology draws devices the way the floor plan does: the plan's switch glyph with its name in small mono type, its own glyph for routers, firewalls and internet lines, on the plan's grid. A device that is down is the red glyph with a cross, as on the plan.
- Links run at right angles along a shared trunk and bus, with a count where several share a run, so ten firewalls under the core no longer cross each other. A small network no longer blows up to fill the page.
