### Changed

- Inventory's feature switches no longer save the moment they are clicked: like properties, telemetry
  setup and a group's, tag's or entry's details, changes collect in the save bar ("2 unsaved changes",
  Save, Discard changes), the same bar Settings, an agent's settings and a plugin's per-agent
  settings use.
- Closing or reloading the tab with unsaved changes asks first on an agent's settings, a plugin's
  per-agent settings and inventory pages too, not only on Settings.
