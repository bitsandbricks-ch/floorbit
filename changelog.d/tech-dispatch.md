### Added

- Tech dispatch: **Send to nearest tech** on the incident board gives an incident to the nearest
  free tech on duty, by the floor plan. Techs get **My jobs** (`/jobs`) on their phone: go on or
  off duty, say where they are, see each job's seat on the hall plan with a line from where they
  are, and mark it "on my way", done or give it back. See `docs/dispatch.md`.
